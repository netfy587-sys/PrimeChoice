How to compare products before you buy

A good purchase starts before you open a shopping site. Decide what you need, learn how to read reviews, and compare the full cost, not just the price tag.

1. Write down what you actually need

List three must-have features and one maximum budget before you browse. This stops flashy extras from pulling you toward a product you do not need.

2. Read reviews critically

Look at the middle ratings first, because they are usually the most detailed. Be careful when many reviews appear on the same day, use similar wording, or only praise without mentioning any use over time.

  • Prefer reviews that describe how long the product was used.
  • Check independent testing sites as well as shop reviews.

3. Compare the total cost

Add delivery, accessories, subscriptions, repairs and running costs. A cheaper product that needs expensive refills can cost more over two years.

4. Check warranty and returns

Find out how long the warranty lasts, what it covers, and who pays for return shipping. Keep the receipt and take photos of the packaging when the item arrives.

5. Wait before you buy

For anything non-essential, wait 24 hours. If you still want it and it still fits your list, you are more likely to be happy with the decision.

Questions to ask before checkout

  • Can I return it, and for how many days?
  • Is the seller the brand or a third party?
  • Are spare parts available later?
  • Does the price include tax and delivery?

Decode common marketing claims

Claims are not lies, but they need context.

ClaimWhat to check
Up to 50% offLook at the price history. Only the best item may be 50% off.
Best sellerIn which category, over what period, and who counted?
Lab testedWhich lab, and was it independent of the seller?
Limited time offerDoes the timer reset when you reload the page?
Lifetime warrantyWhose lifetime: yours, the product, or the company?

New, refurbished or second-hand?

Each option suits a different priority.

New
Full warranty and latest model. Highest price.
Refurbished
Tested and restored, usually with a warranty. Ask for at least 12 months of cover.
Second-hand
Lowest price but little protection. Inspect in person and check the serial number.

Spot the dark patterns

Some websites are designed to rush you. Watch for:

  • Fake countdown timers and "only 2 left" messages with no proof.
  • Pre-ticked boxes that add insurance or subscriptions.
  • Fees that only appear on the last step of checkout.
  • Wording that shames you for declining, such as "No, I do not like saving money".

Frequently asked questions

How can I spot fake product reviews?

Look for repeated phrases, many reviews posted at once, and reviewers who only ever leave five-star ratings.

Is the cheapest option usually the best value?

Not always. Value depends on lifespan, running costs and support, so compare the cost over the whole time you will use it.
